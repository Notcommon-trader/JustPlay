# Privacy Policy — JustPlay

**Last updated: 15 August 2026**

> **This describes the app as it exists today, which collects nothing.** That is
> not a permanent promise about the future — it is an accurate statement about
> this version. Ads and analytics are planned. On the day either one ships, this
> document and the Data Safety declaration must both be updated *before* the
> release goes out, because a privacy policy that describes an older, more
> private version of the app is a policy violation, not a stale document.

---

## The short version

JustPlay does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no
account system, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics or
advertising. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

## What is stored on your device

Your best scores, fastest times, number of games played, and when you last played
each game. This is written to your phone's own storage using the standard Android
preferences system.

It never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What is not collected

- No name, email address, or phone number
- No account, because there is no sign-in
- No location
- No contacts, photos, files, or camera access
- No advertising identifier
- No analytics, crash reports, or usage tracking
- No device identifiers of any kind

## Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. The only entry in its manifest is a
standard text-processing query the Flutter framework declares for all apps; it
grants no access to your data.

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works identically with the device in
aeroplane mode.

## Children

JustPlay is not directed at children under 13. It is rated for ages 13 and up and
is not distributed through Google Play's Families programme. Since the app
collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

## Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no cloud
services, no third-party SDKs beyond the open-source Flutter framework and its
preferences plugin, neither of which transmits anything.

## Your data rights

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or
delete on your behalf. To erase everything the app has stored, uninstall it.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, analytics, or accounts, this policy will be
updated and the change will be described in the release notes before that version
ships.
